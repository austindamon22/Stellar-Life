      Why it stops at iron

      Binding energy per nucleon. Moving up the curve releases energy. Light nuclei fuse uphill; heavy nuclei split uphill. Both meet at the iron peak.

      Making gold and platinum

      Fusion stops at iron. Everything heavier is built by neutron capture: a nucleus absorbs neutrons (no electric repulsion to fight), then a neutron turns into a proton by β⁻ decay, moving it up one element.

      Spacetime and proper time

      General relativity: mass curves spacetime, and objects follow the curvature. The sheet shows the curved spatial geometry (an embedding diagram). The colour shows the other half, which matters more for everyday gravity: proper time runs slower deeper in the well.

      time normaltime slowest

      dτ/dt = √(1 − rs/r)   outside the mass  ·  rs = 2GM/c²

      Inside a star the app uses the uniform-density interior solution: dτ/dt = 1.5√(1 − rs/R) − 0.5√(1 − rsr²/R³). Real stars are centrally concentrated, so this is approximate.

      You live with this every day: GPS satellite clocks run ~45 μs/day fast from weaker gravity and ~7 μs/day slow from orbital speed. Without the ~38 μs/day correction, positions would drift ~10 km per day.
